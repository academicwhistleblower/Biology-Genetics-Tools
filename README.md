### Core Areas Covered in Bio 220:

1. **Ecosystem Structure & Energy Flow**
   - Primary productivity calculations ($GPP$ & $NPP$).

2. **Biogeochemical Cycles**
   - Transformation pathways for Nitrogen, Carbon, Phosphorus, and Hydrologic systems.

3. **Population Dynamics & Quantitative Modeling**
   - Exponential vs. logistic growth models ($r$ and $K$ selection).
